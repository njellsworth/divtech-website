Frequently Asked Questions

How quickly will I see results?

Most clients see their first qualified meetings within 2–3 weeks of launch. Full pipeline momentum typically builds over 60–90 days.

Can I change plans later?

Absolutely. You can upgrade or downgrade at the start of any new billing month with no penalties or fees.

What industries do you work with?

We've served B2B companies across SaaS, iPaaS, HR Software, ETL, UX Research, Manufacturing, and more.

Do you guarantee meetings?

We don't sell empty guarantees — we show you our average results by industry. We do guarantee full transparency on every metric.

What tools do you use?

We work with best-in-class tools including Apollo, LinkedIn Sales Navigator, Zoominfo, and more to guarantee a targeted, agile launch and demonstrate results as quickly as possible.

Is there an onboarding fee?

No. There is no setup fee or onboarding charge on any plan. You only pay the monthly rate.
